# Vision TV: Wi-Fi and Internet Problems

## TV won't connect to Wi-Fi

1. Check that other devices in your home can use the internet.
2. Restart the TV and the router: unplug both from power for 60 seconds, then plug them back in.
3. Forget the Wi-Fi network and connect again in Settings > Network > Wi-Fi.
4. Move the router closer to the TV, or connect the TV with an Ethernet cable for the most reliable connection.
5. Reset the network settings in Settings > Network > Reset network settings.

## Error codes

| Code | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| E-104 | Wrong Wi-Fi password | Enter the password again. Passwords are case-sensitive. |
| E-207 | Connected to Wi-Fi but can't reach the internet | Restart the router, then go to Settings > Network > DNS and choose Automatic. |
| E-310 | Kestrel OS servers are unavailable | This is usually temporary. Try again in 30 minutes. |

## Update the TV software

Go to Settings > System > Software update.

If the TV can't connect to the internet, you can update it from a USB drive:

1. Download the latest software for your TV model from the Kestrel support site.
2. Copy the file to a USB drive formatted as FAT32.
3. Plug the USB drive into the TV.
4. Go to Settings > System > Software update > Update from USB.

Do not turn off the TV while it is updating.

## Streaming apps keep buffering

4K streaming needs an internet speed of 25 Mbps or faster. Try a lower video quality in the streaming app, or connect the TV with an Ethernet cable.
