# Pulse Buds Pro: Pairing and Connection Problems

## Pair with a new device

1. Put both earbuds in the charging case and leave the lid open.
2. Press and hold the button on the back of the case for 3 seconds, until the light flashes white.
3. On your phone or computer, open Bluetooth settings and select "Kestrel Pulse Buds Pro".

## Earbuds won't connect

- Make sure the earbuds have at least 10% battery.
- In your device's Bluetooth settings, select "Forget this device" for Pulse Buds Pro, then pair again.
- Turn Bluetooth off and on again.
- The earbuds can connect to 2 devices at the same time. If they are already connected to 2 devices, disconnect one first.

## Only one earbud plays sound

1. Put both earbuds in the case, close the lid, wait 10 seconds, and take them out again.
2. If that doesn't help, reset the earbuds: put both in the case with the lid open, then press and hold the case button for 15 seconds until the light flashes amber 3 times. Pair them with your device again.
3. Clean the metal charging contacts on the earbuds and inside the case with a dry cotton swab.

## Sound cuts out

- Keep your device within 10 meters of the earbuds.
- Update the firmware in the Kestrel Sound app under Settings > Firmware.
- Turn off "High-quality audio mode" in the app. It uses more Bluetooth bandwidth and can cut out in crowded places.

## Still not working?

If the problem continues after a reset and a firmware update, start a warranty claim.
