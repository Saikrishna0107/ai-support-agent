# Aero 14: Battery and Charging Problems

## Laptop stops charging at 80%

This is Battery Care mode, which protects the battery's long-term health. To charge to 100%, open Kestrel Control Center > Power and turn off Battery Care.

## Battery drains quickly

- Lower the screen brightness. OLED screens use more power to show bright and white content, so dark mode also helps.
- Lower the refresh rate from 90 Hz to 60 Hz in Settings > Display > Advanced display.
- Turn down or turn off the keyboard backlight.
- Check which apps use the most battery in Settings > System > Power & battery.
- Choose the "Best power efficiency" power mode.

## Laptop won't charge

1. Use the included 65 W charger, or another USB-C Power Delivery charger of 45 W or more.
2. Try the other USB-C port.
3. Check the charging light next to the USB-C ports:
   - White: charging
   - Blinking amber: the charger is not supported or is too weak
   - Off: no power is reaching the laptop
4. Do a power reset: unplug the charger, press and hold the power button for 30 seconds, then plug in the charger and turn the laptop on.

## Check battery health

Open Kestrel Control Center > Power > Battery Health. If the battery holds less than 70% of its original capacity within the 2-year warranty, the battery is replaced for free under warranty. With a Kestrel Care plan, the battery is replaced when it falls below 80%.

## Safety warning

If the bottom of the laptop is bulging, the touchpad is being pushed up, or the laptop smells like burning, the battery may be swollen. Stop using the laptop immediately, unplug it, and contact Kestrel support right away.
