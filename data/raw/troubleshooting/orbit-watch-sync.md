# Orbit Watch 2: Syncing and Notification Problems

## Watch is not syncing with your phone

1. Make sure Bluetooth is on and your phone is within 10 meters of the watch.
2. Open the Kestrel Health app and pull down on the home screen to refresh.
3. On Android, let the app run in the background: Settings > Apps > Kestrel Health > Battery > Unrestricted.
4. On iPhone, turn on Background App Refresh: Settings > Kestrel Health > Background App Refresh.
5. Restart the watch (press and hold the top button, then select Restart) and restart your phone.

## Notifications are not showing on the watch

- On Android, give Kestrel Health notification access when the app asks, or in your phone's notification settings.
- On iPhone, open Bluetooth settings, tap the "i" next to the watch, and turn on "Share System Notifications".
- Check that Do Not Disturb is off on the watch.

Remember that iPhone users can read notifications but cannot reply from the watch.

## Re-pair the watch

If syncing still fails, re-pair the watch:

1. In the Kestrel Health app, go to Devices > Orbit Watch 2 > Unpair.
2. On the watch, go to Settings > System > Reset.
3. Pair the watch again in the app.

Any data that had not synced before you reset the watch will be lost.

## Heart rate readings look wrong

- Wear the watch one finger's width above your wrist bone. It should be snug but comfortable.
- Clean the sensor on the back of the watch with a soft, dry cloth.
- Readings can be less accurate during intense workouts with a lot of wrist movement.

The Orbit Watch 2 is not a medical device.
