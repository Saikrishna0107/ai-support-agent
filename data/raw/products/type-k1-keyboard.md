# Kestrel Type K1 Mechanical Keyboard

A wireless mechanical keyboard for Windows and Mac. Price: $119.

## Specifications

- **Layout:** 75% layout with 84 keys (US ANSI)
- **Switches:** Choose Kestrel Brown (tactile) or Kestrel Red (linear) at the same price. The switches are hot-swappable and work with 3-pin and 5-pin MX-style switches.
- **Keycaps:** Double-shot PBT
- **Connections:**
  - Wired USB-C
  - Bluetooth 5.1 for up to 3 devices. Switch between them with Fn + 1, Fn + 2, and Fn + 3.
  - 2.4 GHz wireless with the included dongle. Switch to it with Fn + 4.
- **Battery:** 4,000 mAh. Up to 200 hours with the backlight off, or about 40 hours with the RGB backlight on.
- **Mac and Windows:** Press Fn + M for Mac layout or Fn + W for Windows layout.
- **Software:** Kestrel Keys app for Windows and macOS, for remapping keys and changing the lighting
- **Weight:** 820 g

## In the box

Keyboard, USB-C cable, 2.4 GHz dongle (stored in a slot under the keyboard), switch puller, keycap puller, and 4 spare switches.

## Warranty

1-year limited warranty. Swapping switches does not void the warranty, but damage caused while swapping them is not covered.
