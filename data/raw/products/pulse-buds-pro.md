# Kestrel Pulse Buds Pro

Wireless noise-canceling earbuds. Price: $149. Colors: Black, White, and Sage.

## Specifications

- **Noise control:** Active noise cancellation (ANC) and transparency mode
- **Battery:** Up to 8 hours with ANC on, or up to 10 hours with ANC off. Up to 32 hours in total with the charging case.
- **Quick charge:** 5 minutes of charging gives about 1 hour of listening
- **Charging case:** Charges with USB-C or a Qi wireless charger
- **Bluetooth:** Version 5.4, with multipoint connection to 2 devices at the same time
- **Water resistance:** IPX4, which protects against sweat and splashes. Do not wear them swimming or in the shower. The charging case is not water resistant.
- **Controls:** Touch controls (tap, double-tap, press and hold), customizable in the app
- **Microphones:** 3 per earbud
- **Weight:** 5.2 g per earbud
- **App:** Kestrel Sound app for iOS 16 or later and Android 10 or later. Use it to adjust the sound (EQ), change controls, and update firmware.

## In the box

Earbuds, charging case, ear tips in sizes S, M, and L (size M is pre-installed), and a USB-C cable. A power adapter is not included.

## Lost an earbud or the case?

You can buy a single replacement earbud for $49 or a replacement charging case for $39. Contact support to order one.

## Returns

For hygiene reasons, opened Pulse Buds Pro can only be returned if they are defective. Unopened earbuds can be returned within the normal return window.

## Warranty

1-year limited warranty.
