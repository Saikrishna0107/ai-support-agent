# Kestrel Orbit Watch 2

A smartwatch for fitness, health tracking, and notifications.

## Sizes and prices

| Size | Display | Band width | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| 41 mm | 1.4-inch AMOLED | 20 mm | $249 |
| 45 mm | 1.6-inch AMOLED | 22 mm | $279 |

Colors: Midnight, Silver, and Rose Gold. Both sizes use quick-release bands, so any standard band of the right width fits.

## Specifications

- **Sensors:** Heart rate, blood oxygen (SpO2), skin temperature, accelerometer, gyroscope, and barometer
- **GPS:** Dual-frequency GPS
- **Water resistance:** 5 ATM. Safe for swimming in a pool or the ocean. Not for diving, water skiing, saunas, or hot showers.
- **Battery:** Up to 7 days of typical use, up to 2 days with the always-on display, or up to 20 hours of continuous GPS
- **Charging:** Magnetic charger with USB-C cable. Charges from 0 to 100% in 90 minutes.
- **App:** Kestrel Health app

## Phone compatibility

Works with Android 10 or later and iOS 16 or later. Replying to messages and taking calls on the watch work on Android only. iPhone users can read notifications on the watch but cannot reply.

## Health readings

The Orbit Watch 2 is not a medical device. Its health readings are for general wellness and fitness only.

## In the box

Watch, band, magnetic charger with USB-C cable, and a quick start guide.

## Warranty

1-year limited warranty.
