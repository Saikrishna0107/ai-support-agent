# Kestrel Aero 14 Laptop

A thin and light 14-inch laptop for work, study, and creative projects.

## Models and prices

| Model | Memory | Storage | Touch screen | Operating system | Price |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Aero 14 | 16 GB | 512 GB SSD | No | Windows 11 Home | $1,099 |
| Aero 14 Pro | 32 GB | 1 TB SSD | Yes | Windows 11 Pro | $1,399 |

Colors: Graphite and Silver.

## Specifications

- **Display:** 14-inch OLED, 2880 × 1800, 90 Hz refresh rate, 500 nits peak brightness
- **Processor:** 8 cores, 16 threads, up to 4.8 GHz
- **Memory:** Soldered to the motherboard, so it cannot be upgraded
- **Storage:** One M.2 2280 NVMe slot, upgradeable up to 4 TB
- **Battery:** 70 Wh. Up to 14 hours of video playback or up to 10 hours of web browsing.
- **Charging:** 65 W USB-C charger included. Charges from 0 to 50% in 30 minutes.
- **Ports:** 2 × USB-C (USB4, both support charging and display output), 1 × USB-A 3.2, 1 × HDMI 2.1, microSD card reader, 3.5 mm headphone jack
- **External displays:** Up to 3 at once, one on each USB-C port and one on HDMI
- **Wireless:** Wi-Fi 7 and Bluetooth 5.4
- **Camera:** 1080p webcam with privacy shutter, plus an IR camera for face sign-in
- **Weight and size:** 1.3 kg (2.9 lb), 15.9 mm thick

## In the box

Laptop, 65 W USB-C charger with a 1.8 m cable, and a quick start guide.

## Charging with other chargers

The Aero 14 charges with any USB-C Power Delivery charger of 45 W or more, but chargers below 65 W charge more slowly. Chargers under 45 W show a "slow charger" warning and may not charge the laptop while it is in use.

## Battery Care mode

Battery Care mode stops charging at 80% to extend the battery's lifespan. It is off by default. Turn it on in Kestrel Control Center > Power.

## Warranty

2-year limited warranty. Upgrading the SSD does not void the warranty.
