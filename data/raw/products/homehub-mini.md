# Kestrel HomeHub Mini

A compact smart speaker with a voice assistant and smart home controls. Price: $79. Colors: Charcoal and Chalk.

## Specifications

- **Sound:** 360-degree sound from a full-range driver and a passive radiator
- **Wireless:** Wi-Fi 6 (2.4 GHz and 5 GHz) and Bluetooth 5.3
- **Voice assistant:** Kestrel Assistant. Say "Hey Kestrel" to start.
- **Smart home:** Works as a Matter controller and a Thread border router
- **Privacy:** A microphone mute switch on the back. The light turns red when the microphones are off.
- **Stereo pairing:** Pair two HomeHub Minis as left and right speakers in the Kestrel Home app
- **Power:** 15 W power adapter included. The HomeHub Mini cannot be powered over USB.
- **Setup:** Kestrel Home app for iOS 16 or later and Android 10 or later

## Factory reset

Press and hold the Action button and the Volume Down button together for 15 seconds, until the light turns orange. The speaker restarts and is ready to set up again.

## In the box

HomeHub Mini, 15 W power adapter, and a quick start guide.

## Warranty

1-year limited warranty.
