# Kestrel Skyline Drone

A camera drone for aerial photos and video.

## Options and prices

| Option | What's included | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Skyline | Drone, 1 flight battery, remote controller, 2 spare pairs of propellers | $799 |
| Skyline Fly More Combo | Drone, 3 flight batteries, remote controller, battery charging hub, carrying bag, 4 spare pairs of propellers | $999 |

## Specifications

- **Camera:** 4K video at 60 frames per second, 48 MP photos, 3-axis gimbal
- **Flight time:** Up to 34 minutes per battery in calm conditions
- **Range:** Video transmission up to 10 km with no obstacles, where allowed by law
- **Wind resistance:** Up to 38 km/h
- **Obstacle sensing:** Forward, backward, and downward. There is no sideways obstacle sensing.
- **Return to home:** The drone flies back automatically when the battery is low or the controller signal is lost.
- **Flight battery:** 3,850 mAh, charges in 70 minutes
- **Operating temperature:** 0 to 40 °C
- **App:** Kestrel Fly app for iOS 16 or later and Android 10 or later
- **Weight:** 595 g

## Registration and flying rules

In many countries, drones that weigh more than 250 g must be registered with the aviation authority before they are flown. The Skyline weighs 595 g. Check your local rules before your first flight.

## Shipping

The Skyline and spare flight batteries ship by Standard or Express only. Next-Day shipping is not available, and drones cannot be shipped to Canada.

## Returns

- Opened drones that are not defective have a 15% restocking fee.
- Drones that have been flown can only be returned if they are defective.

## Warranty and protection

1-year limited warranty. The warranty does not cover crashes or flyaways. Kestrel Care covers crash damage but not flyaways.
