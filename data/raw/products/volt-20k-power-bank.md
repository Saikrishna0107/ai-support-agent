# Kestrel Volt 20K Power Bank

A portable battery that can charge phones, tablets, and laptops. Price: $59.

## Specifications

- **Capacity:** 20,000 mAh (74 Wh)
- **Ports:**
  - USB-C 1: 65 W input and output (USB Power Delivery)
  - USB-C 2: 20 W output
  - USB-A: 18 W output
- **Maximum total output:** 85 W when using all ports at once
- **Laptop charging:** Charges the Aero 14 laptop at full speed from the USB-C 1 port
- **Recharging:** About 2 hours with a 65 W USB-C charger. A charger is not included.
- **Pass-through charging:** Supported. You can charge a device while the power bank itself is charging.
- **Display:** Shows the remaining battery percentage
- **Weight:** 420 g

## In the box

Power bank and a 60 cm USB-C to USB-C cable.

## Air travel

At 74 Wh, the Volt 20K is under the 100 Wh limit that most airlines allow in carry-on bags. Power banks are never allowed in checked baggage. Always check your airline's rules before you fly.

## Safety

If the power bank is swelling, very hot, or smells like burning, stop using it right away and contact Kestrel support. Do not charge it again.

## Warranty

1-year limited warranty.
