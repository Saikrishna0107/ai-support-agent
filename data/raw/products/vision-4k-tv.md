# Kestrel Vision 4K TV

A 4K QLED smart TV for movies, sports, and gaming.

## Sizes and prices

| Model | Screen size | Wall mount pattern (VESA) | Weight with stand | Price |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Vision 55 | 55 inches | 300 × 300 | 15.8 kg | $649 |
| Vision 65 | 65 inches | 400 × 300 | 23.4 kg | $899 |

A wall mount is not included.

## Specifications

- **Picture:** 4K UHD (3840 × 2160) QLED with a native 120 Hz refresh rate
- **HDR formats:** HDR10, HDR10+, and HLG
- **Gaming:** Variable refresh rate (VRR) and auto low-latency mode (ALLM). 4K at 120 Hz works on HDMI 3 and HDMI 4 only.
- **Ports:** 4 × HDMI (HDMI 2 supports eARC for soundbars), 2 × USB-A, Ethernet, and optical audio out
- **Wireless:** Wi-Fi 6 and Bluetooth 5.2 for wireless headphones
- **Speakers:** 20 W total
- **Smart platform:** Kestrel OS with popular streaming apps
- **Internet speed for 4K streaming:** 25 Mbps or faster recommended

## Delivery

Both sizes ship by freight carrier with free scheduled delivery in 7–10 business days. Express and Next-Day shipping are not available. Vision TVs cannot be shipped to Canada.

## Returns

Opened TVs that are not defective have a 15% restocking fee.

## Pixel defects

During the warranty period, we replace the TV if it has 3 or more defective (stuck bright or dark) pixels, or any defective pixel in the center of the screen.

## Warranty

1-year limited warranty, including the screen panel.
