# Kestrel Limited Warranty

All Kestrel-brand products include a limited warranty against defects in materials and workmanship. The warranty starts on the delivery date.

## Warranty length

| Product | Warranty |
|---|---|
| Aero 14 and Aero 14 Pro laptops | 2 years |
| All other Kestrel products | 1 year |
| Repaired products and replacement parts | The rest of the original warranty or 90 days, whichever is longer |

## What is covered

- Manufacturing defects
- Hardware failures during normal use
- Batteries that hold less than 70% of their original capacity during the warranty period

## What is not covered

- Accidental damage, such as drops, cracked screens, and liquid spills. Kestrel Care covers accidental damage.
- Water damage beyond the product's water-resistance rating
- Normal wear and tear, such as scratches and worn keycaps
- Damage caused by unauthorized repairs or modifications
- Lost or stolen products
- Drone crashes and flyaways

Upgrading the SSD in an Aero 14 laptop or swapping switches on a Type K1 keyboard does not void the warranty. However, damage caused while doing so is not covered.

## How to make a warranty claim

1. Go to the Warranty Claims page in your account. Guests can use the Warranty Claims form on the support site.
2. Enter your order number, the product's serial number, and a description of the problem. Photos or videos help us resolve claims faster.
3. Our team replies within 2 business days, usually with troubleshooting steps first.
4. If the product needs service, we email you a free prepaid shipping label. We repair or replace the product within 10 business days of receiving it and ship it back to you for free.

If a product cannot be repaired and the same model is no longer available, we replace it with a product of equal or better specifications.
