# Holiday Returns

Items purchased between November 1 and December 24 can be returned until January 31 of the following year. For example, a laptop bought on November 28, 2026 can be returned until January 31, 2027.

## Which window applies?

The holiday window only applies when it gives you more time. If 30 days after delivery is later than January 31, the normal 30-day window applies instead. For example, a backordered item bought on December 22 and delivered on January 8 can be returned until February 7.

## Other rules still apply

All other return rules still apply during the holiday window, including condition requirements, the 15% restocking fee on opened TVs and drones, and the special rules for flown drones, opened earbuds, and gift cards.

## Returning a gift

If you received a Kestrel item as a gift, you can return it using the order number printed on the gift receipt. Gift returns are refunded to a Kestrel gift card, not to the original buyer's payment method, and the buyer is not notified.

## Kestrel Care plans

Kestrel Care plans bought during the holiday period follow the normal Kestrel Care cancellation rules.
