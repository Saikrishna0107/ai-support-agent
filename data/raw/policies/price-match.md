# Price Match and Price Protection

## Price protection (Kestrel price drops)

If Kestrel lowers the price of an item within 14 days after you buy it, we refund the difference. Use the "Request price adjustment" button on your Orders page, or contact support. Each item can get one price adjustment.

## Matching other retailers

We match the price of an identical item sold by a major national retailer in the United States. The item must be the same model, condition, color, and capacity, and it must be in stock. You can request a match when you buy or within 14 days after.

Price matching does not apply to:

- Third-party marketplace sellers
- Open-box, refurbished, or used items
- Bundles, membership-only prices, coupons, and mail-in rebates
- Black Friday and Cyber Monday deals (from the Friday after US Thanksgiving through the following Monday)
- Clearance and limited-quantity "doorbuster" deals
- Prices from Canadian retailers

## How to request a price match

Contact support by chat or phone and share a link to the competitor's product page. Price matches are limited to one per item and to 5 units of the same product per customer.

## How you get the money back

Price matches and price adjustments are refunded to your original payment method. Banks usually take 5–7 business days to show the refund.
