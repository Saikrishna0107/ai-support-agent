# Shipping Policy

Kestrel Electronics ships to all 50 US states and to Canada. We do not ship to other countries at this time.

## Processing time

Orders placed before 2:00 PM Eastern Time (ET) on a business day (Monday to Friday, excluding US federal holidays) usually ship the same day. Orders placed after 2:00 PM ET, or on weekends and holidays, ship the next business day.

## Shipping options (United States)

| Option | Delivery time after shipping | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 5–7 business days | $5.99, or free on orders of $50 or more |
| Express | 2–3 business days | $14.99 |
| Next-Day | 1 business day | $29.99 |

- The $50 free-shipping threshold is based on the order subtotal after discounts and before tax.
- Next-Day orders placed after 2:00 PM ET on Friday, or on a weekend, ship on Monday and arrive on Tuesday.
- Next-Day shipping is not available to Alaska, Hawaii, or US territories.
- Express and Next-Day shipping cannot be delivered to PO boxes.
- Drones and spare drone batteries can only ship by Standard or Express, never Next-Day.
- Kestrel Rewards Gold members get free Express shipping on every US order. See the Kestrel Rewards page.

## Large items (TVs 55 inches and larger)

TVs 55 inches and larger ship by freight carrier with free scheduled delivery, which takes 7–10 business days. The carrier will call or text you to choose a 4-hour delivery window. Express and Next-Day shipping are not available for these items. Someone 18 or older must be present to sign for the delivery.

## Shipping to Canada

- Standard shipping only: 7–12 business days, $19.99, or free on orders of $150 USD or more.
- All prices are charged in US dollars.
- Canadian customers pay import duties and taxes when the package is delivered. These are not included in our prices and Kestrel cannot refund them.
- TVs and drones cannot be shipped to Canada.

## Tracking your order

You will receive an email with a tracking number when your order ships. Tracking also appears on the Orders page of your account. It can take up to 24 hours for tracking information to update after the shipping email is sent.
