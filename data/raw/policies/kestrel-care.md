# Kestrel Care Protection Plans

Kestrel Care is an optional protection plan. It extends coverage beyond the standard warranty and adds protection against accidental damage.

## Plan prices

The plan price depends on the product's purchase price.

| Product price | 2-year plan | 3-year plan |
|---|---|---|
| Under $100 | $9.99 | $14.99 |
| $100 to $499.99 | $39.99 | $59.99 |
| $500 to $1,499.99 | $99.99 | $149.99 |
| $1,500 and up | $199.99 | $279.99 |

The plan starts on the product's delivery date and includes the standard warranty period. For example, a 3-year plan on an Aero 14 laptop covers years 1 and 2 together with the 2-year warranty, and year 3 on its own.

## What's covered

- Everything the standard warranty covers, for the full length of the plan
- Accidental damage, including drops, cracked screens, liquid spills, and drone crashes
- Battery replacement when the battery holds less than 80% of its original capacity
- Up to 2 accidental damage claims in any 12-month period

## Service fees

- Mechanical or electrical failures: no fee
- Accidental damage claims: $49 per claim

## What's not covered

- Loss and theft
- Drone flyaways, where the drone is lost and not recovered
- Cosmetic damage that does not affect how the product works
- Intentional damage

## Buying a plan

- Add Kestrel Care at checkout, or later from the Orders page up to 60 days after the product's purchase date.
- Kestrel Care is not available for gift cards or for accessories priced under $20.
- Kestrel Care is available in the United States only.

## Canceling a plan

- Cancel within 30 days of buying the plan for a full refund.
- After 30 days, you get a prorated refund for the unused months, minus the cost of any claims we have paid.
- If you return the product, its plan is canceled automatically and fully refunded.

## Making a claim

Start a claim from the Kestrel Care page in your account, or call support. Claims follow the same process as warranty claims: we email a prepaid shipping label and repair or replace the product within 10 business days of receiving it.
