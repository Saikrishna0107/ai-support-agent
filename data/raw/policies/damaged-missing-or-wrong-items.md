# Damaged, Missing, or Wrong Items

## Item damaged in shipping

Report shipping damage within 48 hours of delivery. Go to Orders, select "Report a problem", and upload photos of the outer box, the packaging, and the damaged item. Keep all packaging until the issue is resolved.

Once we approve the report, we ship a free replacement by Express within 1 business day and email you a prepaid label to send back the damaged item. If the item is out of stock, we refund you instead.

**TV deliveries:** Check the TV for visible damage while the delivery driver is there. If you see damage, write it on the carrier's delivery receipt or refuse the delivery. Report hidden damage found after unpacking within 48 hours.

## Wrong item received

Report a wrong item within 30 days of delivery. We ship the correct item for free and email a prepaid label for the wrong item, which you must send back within 14 days.

## Package marked delivered but not received

1. Wait 24 hours. Carriers sometimes mark packages as delivered before they arrive.
2. Check around your property, with neighbors, and with your building's mailroom.
3. If you still can't find it, report it within 7 days of the delivery date.

We then open an investigation with the carrier, which takes up to 5 business days. If the package is not found, we send a replacement or a refund.

## Item missing from a package

First check your Orders page: your order may have shipped in more than one package. If the item is not in another package, report it within 7 days of delivery.
