# Payment Methods

## Accepted payment methods

- Credit and debit cards: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover
- PayPal
- Apple Pay and Google Pay
- Kestrel gift cards (up to 2 gift cards per order)
- Kestrel Rewards certificates
- Pay in 4 installments

We do not accept cash on delivery, checks, money orders, or cryptocurrency.

## When you are charged

Your card is authorized when you place the order and charged when the order ships. If your order ships in more than one package, each package is charged separately. If you cancel before the order ships, your bank releases the authorization hold within 3–5 business days.

## Pay in 4

- Split your purchase into 4 interest-free payments, due every 2 weeks.
- Available for orders from $50 to $2,000.
- The first payment is due at checkout.
- Pay in 4 is provided by our financing partner. Approval depends on an eligibility check, which is a soft credit check that does not affect your credit score.
- Late fees are set by the financing partner, and Kestrel cannot waive them. Contact the financing partner about installment payments. Kestrel support can still help with orders and returns.

## Payment declined

- Check that the billing address matches the address on your card statement.
- Check the card's expiry date and security code.
- Your bank may have blocked the purchase. Contact your bank to approve it.
- After 3 declined attempts, checkout is locked for 1 hour for security.

## Billing problems

If you see a charge you don't recognize, or you think you were charged twice, contact our billing team by phone. Billing disputes are always handled by a human agent.
