# Changing or Canceling an Order

## Order statuses

| Status | What it means | Can you cancel? | Can you change the address? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Placed | We have received your order | Yes | Yes |
| Processing | We are packing your order | Yes | Yes |
| Shipped | Your order is on its way | No, return it instead | No |
| Delivered | Your order has arrived | No, return it instead | No |

## Canceling an order

Go to Orders in your account and select "Cancel order". You can cancel any item that has not shipped yet, and cancellation is free. Your bank releases the payment authorization within 3–5 business days.

## Changing the shipping address

You can change the address on the Orders page until the order ships. After it ships, Kestrel cannot change the address. You may be able to redirect the package with the carrier using your tracking number, but the carrier may charge a fee.

## Changing items

We cannot add items to an order or swap items after the order is placed. Cancel the item you don't want and place a new order.

## After your order ships

If you no longer want an order that has shipped, you can refuse the delivery so the package returns to us, or return it after it arrives. Refused packages are refunded within 5–7 business days after they reach our warehouse, minus the original shipping cost.

## Preorders

You can cancel a preorder at any time before it ships. Your card is not charged until the preorder ships.
