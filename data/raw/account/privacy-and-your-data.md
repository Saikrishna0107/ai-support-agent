# Privacy and Your Data

## What we collect

- Your name, contact details, and shipping addresses
- Your order history
- Payment information. Our payment processor stores it, and Kestrel never stores full card numbers.
- Device and browsing information from our website
- Support conversations

## How we use it

We use your information to process orders, provide support, prevent fraud, and, if you opt in, send marketing emails. We do not sell your personal information.

## Support chat records

Chat conversations, including conversations with our AI assistant, are kept for 12 months to improve our service and then deleted.

## Your choices

- **Marketing emails:** Unsubscribe with the link in any marketing email or in Account > Notifications. Order and shipping emails cannot be turned off.
- **Download your data:** Go to Account > Privacy > Request my data. We email you a download link within 30 days.
- **Delete your account:** Go to Account > Privacy > Delete account. We process deletion requests within 30 days.

## What happens when you delete your account

- Order and payment records are kept for 7 years to meet tax and legal requirements.
- Unused Rewards points and certificates are lost.
- Gift card balances are not affected, because gift cards are not linked to accounts.
