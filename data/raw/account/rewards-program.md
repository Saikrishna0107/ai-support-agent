# Kestrel Rewards

Kestrel Rewards is our free loyalty program. Join from the Account page.

## Earning points

- Members earn 1 point for every $1 spent on eligible purchases. Points are based on the order subtotal after discounts, before tax and shipping.
- Gold members earn 1.5 points for every $1.
- Gift cards, Kestrel Care plans, and shipping fees do not earn points.
- Points appear in your account 14 days after the order is delivered.
- If you return an item, the points you earned on it are removed.

## Using points

- Every 100 points automatically turn into a $5 Rewards certificate.
- Rewards certificates expire 90 days after they are issued.
- Rewards certificates cannot be used to buy gift cards and have no cash value.

## Gold tier

Spend $1,000 or more on eligible purchases in one calendar year to reach Gold. Gold status lasts for the rest of that year and the whole next calendar year.

Gold benefits:

- 1.5 points for every $1 spent
- Free Express shipping on all US orders
- Free return shipping labels
- A priority phone line

## Points expiration

All points expire if your account has no purchases for 12 months in a row.
