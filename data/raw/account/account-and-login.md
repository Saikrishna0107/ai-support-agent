# Account and Login Help

## Creating an account

You need an email address and a password of at least 10 characters. You can also check out as a guest. To link past guest orders to a new account, sign up with the same email address: orders placed with that email in the last 2 years are added automatically.

## Forgot your password

Select "Forgot password" on the sign-in page. The reset link is valid for 30 minutes. If the email doesn't arrive within 10 minutes, check your spam folder. You can request a new link up to 5 times per hour.

## Account locked

After 5 incorrect password attempts, sign-in is locked for 15 minutes. Wait 15 minutes or reset your password.

## Two-step verification

Turn on two-step verification in Account > Security. You can use an authenticator app or text messages (SMS). When you turn it on, you get 10 one-time backup codes. Keep them somewhere safe.

If you lose access to your phone, sign in with a backup code. If you have no backup codes, contact support. We will verify your identity, which can take up to 2 business days.

Kestrel support will never ask for your password or a verification code.

## Changing your email address

Go to Account > Profile. We send a confirmation link to the new email address, and the link is valid for 24 hours.

## Deleting your account

See the Privacy and Your Data page.
