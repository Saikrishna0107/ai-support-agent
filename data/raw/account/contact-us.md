# Contact Kestrel Support

## Ways to reach us

| Channel | Hours (Eastern Time) | Typical response |
|---|---|---|
| Chat with our AI assistant | 24 hours a day, 7 days a week | Instant |
| Chat with a human agent | 8 AM to 10 PM, every day | Under 5 minutes |
| Phone: 1-800-555-0142 | Mon–Fri 9 AM to 8 PM; Sat–Sun 10 AM to 6 PM | Under 10 minutes |
| Email: support@kestrel-electronics.example | Any time | Within 24 hours |

Gold members can call the priority phone line at 1-800-555-0199.

Phone and human chat support are closed on New Year's Day, Thanksgiving Day, and Christmas Day. The AI assistant is always available.

## When you are connected to a human agent

Our AI assistant answers most questions. It transfers you to a human agent when:

- You ask to speak with a person
- The issue involves a billing dispute, an unrecognized charge, or suspected fraud
- There is a safety issue, such as a battery that is swelling, overheating, smoking, or smells like burning
- You are asking for an exception to our policies
- The assistant cannot find a confident answer

Outside human chat hours, the assistant creates a support ticket and a human agent replies by email within 24 hours.

## Safety first

If a device is swelling, smoking, very hot, or smells like burning:

1. Stop using it and unplug it.
2. If it is safe to do so, move it away from anything flammable.
3. Do not charge it again.
4. Contact us right away. Do not ship it yourself. We will send instructions for a safe return or disposal.

## Complaints and escalation

Ask any agent to escalate your case to a supervisor. Escalated cases get a reply within 1 business day and are resolved within 5 business days.
